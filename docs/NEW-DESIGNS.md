# 35 new card designs (11 Nov 2026)

From the user's Loom on the design library. What makes a design good:

- **The middle stays empty.** Text needs most of the card.
- **Few elements, but very intricate.** Small, detailed, beautiful pieces carry the card: a garland across the top, a pair of elephants at the bottom, pillars at the sides.
- **Not crowded, no "zoo".** Birds everywhere or horses buried in flowers are out.
- **One solid background colour.** Two-tone or see-through backgrounds caused problems.
- Liked examples: maroon with a marigold curtain and carved pillars; cream with marigold garlands at the top and bottom; maroon with a pair of horses at the bottom; the plain Haldi arch with small marigold corners; peacocks small at the base; white elephants at the bottom under chandeliers.
- Wanted: more elephants, tigers, regional and cultural styles, abstract and painted pieces, subtle prints.

## The shared brief (added to every prompt)

> Vertical Indian wedding invitation card background. A large plain empty centre, about 70% of the card, in one smooth flat solid colour with no texture or pattern, kept completely clear for text. Decoration only at the edges. Few elements, but intricate, delicate, hand-painted detail. Elegant, premium, minimal. No text, no letters, no people.
