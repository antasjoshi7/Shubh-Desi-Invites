## What this changed (1 Nov 2026)

- The design gallery moved to `/wedding-card-design` and now targets the biggest cluster. It shows
  finished sample cards first (people searching "card design" want to see complete cards, not blank
  backgrounds), answers "how do I design a wedding card for free?", and covers Hindu and shadi cards.
- The e-card page moved to `/create-marriage-invitation-card` and targets "create marriage invitation card".
- The home page title and copy now cover the invite-video cluster (invite/invitation/marriage/card video)
  and "video templates".
