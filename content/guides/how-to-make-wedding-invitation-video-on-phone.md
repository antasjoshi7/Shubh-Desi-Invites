You can make a full Indian wedding invitation video on your phone in about five minutes, for free, without installing an app or signing up. This guide walks through it with the [free wedding invitation video maker](/) on this site, from the first page to sending it on WhatsApp.

## What you need

- Your phone (Android or iPhone) with Chrome or Safari. A computer works too.
- The details for each function: date, time, venue and dress code.
- The names as they should appear, including parents' names if you're adding them.

## Step 1: Open the editor and pick a design

Open the [invite maker](/create). You start with an intro page. Tap **Design** at the bottom and choose artwork: there are sections for Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, wedding and reception, plus peacocks, elephants, palaces and more. Every design keeps a large plain space in the middle, so your text never sits on the artwork.

Tip: pick one design family for the whole invite (all palace arches, or all marigolds). It looks like a printed card set.

## Step 2: Type your names and details

Tap any line of text on the page once to pick the whole block, then tap a line again to change it. Type, and the page updates as you go. Text shrinks itself to fit the design, so long names and addresses still fit.

To make a line bigger or smaller, pick it and use **Size**, or pinch it on the page. To move the whole block, drag it.

## Step 3: Add a page for each function

Tap **+** in the row of pages and choose **Invitation** for the main invite, then one page per event: **Haldi**, **Mehendi**, **Sangeet**, **Shaadi** and **Reception**. Each event page comes with sample wording for that event; change the date, time, venue and dress code.

Need words? Copy them from the [wedding invitation wording](/wedding-invitation-wording) collection, or from the event pages like [Haldi invitation wording](/haldi-invitation-video#wording).

To change the order, hold a page in the row and slide it. Tap the page you're on to copy or delete it.

## Step 4: Add Ganesh ji, Om or your initials

Tap **Symbol** to choose the sign at the top of each page: Ganesh ji or Om in several styles, or a monogram made from the couple's initials. Drag it to move it, or pinch to resize.

## Step 5: Pick music and animation

Tap **Motion** to choose how each page comes alive: rose petals, marigold garlands, diya lights, a baraat horse that gallops across the page, and more. You can also set how long each page shows (3 to 15 seconds).

Tap **Music** and press ▶ to listen to tracks like shehnai and dhol. The music is original, so it won't get muted on Instagram.

Press **Play** at the top to watch the whole invite.

## Step 6: Download and send

Press **Download**, then **Make it move**. The video is made on your phone (nothing is uploaded) and you get a full HD MP4 file with no watermark. Send it on WhatsApp as a Document (📎, then Document) so it stays sharp, with a personal message; there are [ready messages to copy](/wedding-invitation-wording#whatsapp). In the same sheet you can also download the PDF card, where the venue opens the map.

Want a card instead? Open **More**, tap **PDF or pictures**, untick any pages you don't need, and download a print-ready PDF or pictures as an [e-card](/create-marriage-invitation-card).

## Common questions

**My video won't export.** Keep the page open and the screen on until it finishes. On older phones, close other apps first. If it still fails, try Chrome.

**Can I finish later?** Yes. Your invite saves on your phone as you go. Open the editor again on the same phone and browser to carry on.

**How long should the video be?** Most invites are 30 to 45 seconds: an intro, the main invitation, one page per function and a thank-you page.
