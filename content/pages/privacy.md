The short version: **your invite never leaves your phone or computer.** We don't have accounts, a database, or any copy of what you make.

## Your invite

Everything you type (names, dates, venues, phone numbers) and the video you export are made inside your web browser, on your own device. They're never uploaded to us or anyone else.

Your work in progress is saved in your browser's local storage, on your device only, so you can close the page and come back. To delete it, use **More › New invite**, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

## Visit counts

To learn which pages and features are useful, we count visits and a few actions (such as "a design was picked" or "a video was exported") with PostHog, in its cookieless mode. That means:

- no cookies and nothing stored on your device for tracking, so there's no cookie banner;
- no names, text or anything you typed is ever sent, only that an action happened;
- we can't identify you, and we don't sell or share this data with advertisers.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like every web host, Vercel's servers briefly process technical details such as your IP address to deliver the page to you. See [Vercel's privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## Fonts and music

Fonts and music are served from this site itself. No third-party font or music service sees your visit.

## Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.
